01 · KASHI / VARANASI

Kashi Tour

Sacred ghats → Kashi Vishwanath → heritage temples → Sarnath → old-city bazaars

Explore attractions ↓

Major Ghats along the Ganges

  • Dashashwamedh Ghat: Famous for the grand daily evening Ganga Aarti.
  • Assi Ghat: At the southern end of the old riverfront, known for Subah-e-Banaras, sunrise rituals and morning activity.
  • Manikarnika Ghat & Harishchandra Ghat: Historic cremation ghats that remain active sacred spaces in Kashi.
  • Panchganga Ghat & Kedar Ghat: Sacred riverfront stops known for spiritual significance and distinctive architecture.

Prominent Temples & Religious Sites

  • Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple: One of the 12 Jyotirlingas and the spiritual heart of Varanasi.
  • Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple: A major shrine dedicated to Lord Hanuman.
  • New Vishwanath Temple (BHU): A major temple inside the Banaras Hindu University campus.
  • Kaal Bhairav Temple: Dedicated to Kaal Bhairav, traditionally revered as the guardian or Kotwal of Kashi.
  • Durga Kund Mandir & Tulsi Manas Temple: Two important southern Varanasi temples associated with devotion, history and Ramcharitmanas traditions.

Historical & Cultural Landmarks

  • Sarnath: About 10 km from central Varanasi; associated with the Buddha's first sermon and home to Dhamek Stupa and the archaeological museum.
  • Ramnagar Fort: An 18th-century fort and palace complex on the eastern bank of the Ganges.
  • Banaras Hindu University (BHU) & Bharat Kala Bhavan: A landmark university campus with an important museum of Indian art and heritage.
  • Vishwanath Gali & Godowlia Market: Old-city shopping lanes known for Banarasi textiles, worship items, snacks and local street life.

03 · SANGAM & HERITAGE

Prayagraj Sangam Tour

Triveni Sangam → Bade Hanuman → Alopi Devi → Akshaya Vat → Anand Bhavan

Explore attractions ↓

Sacred Pilgrimage Sites

  • Triveni Sangam: The revered confluence of the Ganges, Yamuna and the traditionally believed Saraswati; a major site of the Kumbh and Maha Kumbh.
  • Bade Hanuman Mandir: Known for its unusual reclining idol of Lord Hanuman near the Sangam.
  • Alopi Devi Mandir: A widely revered Shakti shrine in Alopibagh.
  • Shankar Viman Mandapam: A prominent multi-storey South Indian-style temple near the riverfront.
  • Patalpuri Temple & Akshaya Vat: Sacred sites within the Prayagraj Fort area, subject to local access arrangements.

Historical & Cultural Landmarks

  • Prayagraj Fort (Allahabad Fort): A major Mughal fort built during the reign of Emperor Akbar on the Yamuna riverfront.
  • Anand Bhavan & Swaraj Bhavan: Historic buildings associated with the Nehru family and India's freedom movement, now preserved as museums.
  • Chandrashekhar Azad Park: A major public park associated with revolutionary Chandrashekhar Azad's final stand.

04 · RAMAYANA CIRCUIT

Chitrakoot Tour

Ram Ghat → Kamadgiri → Hanuman Dhara → Gupt Godavari → Janaki Kund

Explore attractions ↓

Sacred Pilgrimage Sites & Temples

  • Ram Ghat: The central Mandakini River ghat, known for evening aarti and boat rides.
  • Kamadgiri Hill & Temple: The sacred hill encircled by the traditional parikrama route and closely associated with Lord Rama's exile.
  • Hanuman Dhara: A hilltop Hanuman shrine associated with a natural spring.
  • Sati Anusuya Ashram: A forest hermitage associated with Maharishi Atri and Mata Anusuya.
  • Bharat Milap Temple: Traditionally associated with the meeting of Bharat and Lord Rama.

Caves & Natural Landmarks

  • Gupt Godavari Caves: Natural caves with an underground stream and strong Ramayana associations.
  • Sphatik Shila: Sacred rocks on the Mandakini riverbank associated with Rama and Sita traditions.
  • Janaki Kund: A peaceful river bathing spot traditionally associated with Goddess Sita.
  • Bharat Koop: A sacred well linked to the waters brought by Bharat from major pilgrimage sites.

Historical & Nearby Heritage

  • Ganesh Bagh: A 19th-century architectural complex known for ornate stone work.
  • Ram Darshan: A cultural museum presenting Ramayana values through sculpture and visual displays.
  • Kalinjar Fort: A major historic hill fort in the wider Bundelkhand region that can be added to an extended itinerary.

06 · VEDIC HERITAGE

Naimisharanya Tour

Chakra Tirtha → Lalita Devi → Vyas Gaddi → Sut Gaddi → sacred Gomti circuit

Explore attractions ↓

Sacred Pilgrimage Sites & Kunds

  • Chakra Tirtha: The central sacred water body of Naimisharanya, traditionally associated with Lord Vishnu's Sudarshana Chakra.
  • Dadhichi Kund, Misrikh: A nearby sacred site associated with Maharishi Dadhichi and the tradition of his self-sacrifice.
  • Dashashwamedh Ghat: A sacred Gomti riverfront stop associated in local tradition with Lord Rama and Ashwamedha worship.

Revered Temples

  • Lalita Devi Temple: One of the most revered Shakti shrines of Naimisharanya.
  • Hanuman Garhi: A prominent Hanuman shrine in the pilgrimage area.
  • Shri Balaji Temple: A modern temple complex inspired by South Indian Balaji temple architecture.
  • Rudravart Mahadev: A Shiva pilgrimage site associated with the Gomti River.

Vedic Heritage & Ancient Sites

  • Vyas Gaddi: Traditionally revered as the seat of Maharishi Ved Vyasa and associated with Vedic and Puranic learning.
  • Sut Gaddi: Associated with the narration of the Puranas to assembled sages.
  • Pandav Kila: A site linked in local tradition with the Pandavas and Kunti.
  • Manu & Satarupa Tapasthali: A pilgrimage spot associated with the penance of Manu and Satarupa.

07 · KRISHNA CIRCUIT

Mathura · Vrindavan · Barsana Tour

Mathura → Vrindavan → Govardhan → Radha Kund → Barsana

Explore attractions ↓

Mathura — Birthplace of Lord Krishna

  • Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi Temple: The sacred complex associated with the birthplace of Lord Krishna.
  • Dwarkadhish Temple: One of Mathura's best-known historic temples, noted for vibrant rituals and architecture.
  • Vishram Ghat: The principal Yamuna riverfront ghat, known for evening aarti and boat rides.
  • Gita Mandir (Birla Mandir): A tranquil temple complex noted for inscriptions from the Bhagavad Gita.
  • Government Museum, Mathura: Known for sculpture, coins and archaeological material associated with the Mathura school of art.

Vrindavan — Land of Devotion & Leelas

  • Shri Banke Bihari Temple: One of Vrindavan's most revered Krishna temples, known for its distinctive darshan tradition.
  • Prem Mandir: A large white-stone temple complex known for illuminated evening displays and devotional relief work.
  • ISKCON Krishna Balaram Mandir: Known for kirtan, worship and its marble temple complex.
  • Nidhivan: A deeply revered sacred grove associated with Radha-Krishna traditions.
  • Radha Raman & Radha Vallabh Temples: Historic temples central to Vrindavan's devotional heritage.

Barsana — Radha Rani's Sacred Town

  • Radha Rani Temple (Shriji Temple): The principal hilltop temple of Barsana, with sweeping views of the surrounding Braj landscape.
  • Kirti Mandir: A modern temple dedicated to Mata Kirti and Radha Rani.
  • Maan Mandir: A hilltop devotional site associated with Radha-Krishna traditions.
  • Prem Sarovar: A sacred pond in the Barsana area known for its quiet devotional setting.

Also consider: Extended itineraries can also include Govardhan Hill, Radha Kund and Gokul.

08 · BHAGAVATA HERITAGE

Shukratal Tour

Shukdev Ashram → Akshay Vat → Ganga Ghat → Hanumant Dham → Ganeshdham

Explore attractions ↓

Sacred Heritage & Pilgrimage Sites

  • Shukdev Ashram / Shukdev Temple: The central pilgrimage complex associated with Sage Shukdev and King Parikshit.
  • Akshay Vat: The ancient banyan tree traditionally associated with the narration of the Srimad Bhagavatam.
  • Shukratal Ganga Ghat: The Ganga riverfront used for holy bathing, prayer and pilgrimage rituals.

Prominent Dhams & Shrines

  • Hanumant Dham: Known for its monumental outdoor Hanuman image and devotional complex.
  • Ganeshdham: A popular shrine known for its large outdoor image of Lord Ganesha.
  • Shiv Dham: A Shiva devotional site in the Shukratal pilgrimage area.
  • Durga Dham: A major Goddess Durga shrine within the local sacred circuit.

Gardens & Nearby Stops

  • Nakshatra Vatika: A garden associated with the 27 nakshatras and planted with a variety of trees and medicinal plants.
  • Kargil Shaheed Smarak: A local memorial that may be included as a nearby stop.

09 · HERITAGE & DEVOTION

Agra Tour

Taj Mahal → Agra Fort → Mehtab Bagh → Mankameshwar → traditional bazaars

Explore attractions ↓

UNESCO & Mughal Heritage

  • Taj Mahal: The world-famous white marble mausoleum built by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal.
  • Agra Fort: A vast red-sandstone Mughal fort complex that served as an imperial residence.
  • Mehtab Bagh: A riverside garden across the Yamuna from the Taj Mahal, known for classic views of the monument.

Religious & Nature Stops

  • Mankameshwar Mandir: A highly revered Shiva temple in old Agra near the fort area.
  • Guru Ka Taal: A historic Sikh pilgrimage site associated with Guru Tegh Bahadur.
  • Keetham Lake / Sur Sarovar Bird Sanctuary: A nature and birding stop on the outskirts of Agra.

Traditional Bazaars

  • Sadar Bazaar & Kinari Bazaar: Busy markets for marble inlay work, textiles, leather goods, handicrafts and Agra's famous petha.
